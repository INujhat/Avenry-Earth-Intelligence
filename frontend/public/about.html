Avenry Earth Intelligence

Avenry is a research-stage environmental intelligence platform for exploring Earth observation data alongside the human places and infrastructure that may matter when environmental conditions change.

Launch the interactive investigation workspace

What can visitors explore?

Choose a location and dates, search for NASA Sentinel-1 acquisition records through the Alaska Satellite Facility API, and retrieve nearby mapped facilities using OpenStreetMap-based services. Review records on an interactive MapLibre map, save investigations in your browser, and export source-attributed evidence.

How is the software built?

The public interface uses React, TypeScript, Vite and MapLibre GL. Python research modules use Rasterio and NumPy to compare aligned binary water masks, and Shapely and PyProj to calculate proximity between water-change polygons and mapped facilities. These processing modules are not yet integrated into the production investigation workflow.

Scientific limitations

Satellite acquisition metadata does not prove flooding. Mapped facilities are not verified as damaged or operational. The live public investigation interface does not yet perform validated global flood segmentation, predict emergencies, or calculate population impacts. The project is not an emergency warning service.

Who created Avenry?

Avenry Earth Intelligence is developed by Nujhat Alam Mredula as a software engineering and geospatial research project.
