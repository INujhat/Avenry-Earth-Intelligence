Methodology and evidence limits

PUBLIC RESEARCH DOCUMENTATION

1. NASA satellite acquisition discovery

The investigation interface queries the NASA Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF) API for Sentinel-1 scene metadata intersecting a selected geographic area and date range. Returned acquisition records are evidence of satellite coverage, not direct evidence of a flood or measured water change.

2. Mapped community facilities

The application retrieves nearby OpenStreetMap-based facility records and calculates distances to the selected investigation center. Map completeness and coordinate accuracy vary by region. Facility listings do not verify damage, safety, or service availability.

3. Water-mask comparison module

A separate Python processing module compares two pre-existing binary georeferenced water masks. Pixels coded 0 represent non-water, 1 represent water, and 255 represents invalid data. Inputs must have matching coordinate reference systems, transforms and raster dimensions; invalid pixels are excluded. Outputs include water-gain and water-loss areas, a GeoTIFF, GeoJSON change polygons, and provenance including SHA-256 input hashes.

4. Geometric exposure module

A separate Python module uses Shapely and PyProj to check overlap or proximity between gained-water polygons and mapped facility points. This indicates only potential proximity; it does not establish flooding, damage, closures, casualties, or population exposure.

5. Model validation and production status

A research U-Net segmentation approach is under development. The public investigation workflow does not yet connect raw Sentinel-1 downloads, radiometric preprocessing, production inference, aligned before-and-after predictions and spatial exposure reporting end to end. Reliable model assessment requires event-disjoint testing, IoU, precision, recall, uncertainty reporting and comparison with baseline methods.

Sources and limitations

NASA ASF: Alaska Satellite Facility. Mapping: OpenStreetMap. All environmental findings should be independently validated. Avenry is not an official emergency or hazard-warning service.
